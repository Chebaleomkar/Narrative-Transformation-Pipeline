You are a narrative architect. Given an analysis of a source story and a target world,
your job is to create a TRANSFORMATION PLAN — mapping every narrative element into the new context.

== SOURCE STORY ANALYSIS ==
{{analysis}}

== TARGET WORLD ==
{{target_world}}

== YOUR TASK ==
Create a detailed transformation plan. For each element, explain your mapping rationale.

### 1. WORLD BIBLE (400-500 words)
Establish the target world with concrete detail:
- **Setting**: What does this world look, feel, sound like?
- **Power Structures**: Who has power, how is it gained and lost?
- **Conflict Mechanics**: How do people fight in this world? What weapons are available?
- **Communication**: How do people talk, scheme, and share information?
- **Cultural Rules**: What does this society value? What does it suppress?

### 2. CHARACTER TRANSFORMATIONS
For EVERY character from the analysis, provide:
- **Original** → **New Name** (new identity)
- **New Role**: Their position/identity in this world
- **Why This Works**: How their narrative function translates naturally
- **Voice**: How they talk in this world (1-2 example speech patterns)
- **Appearance**: Brief visual description grounded in this world

### 3. PLOT BEAT REMAPPING
For EVERY plot beat from the analysis, describe:
- **Original Beat** → **New Scene**: How this event plays out in the target world
- **Key Tension**: What makes this scene compelling in the new context

### 4. MOTIF TRANSLATIONS
For EVERY motif from the analysis:
- **Original Symbol** → **New Equivalent**: What carries the same meaning in this world?
- **Usage**: How it appears in the story naturally (not forced)

### 5. THE SOUL — PRESERVED
Explain in 2-3 sentences how the IRREDUCIBLE ESSENCE of the original story
is preserved in this transformation. What makes this unmistakably the same story
despite the new setting?

Be specific and vivid. Avoid generic mappings — every choice should feel inevitable,
not arbitrary. The characters should BELONG in this world, not feel like costumes.
