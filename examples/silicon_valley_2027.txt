AI Startup Ecosystem, San Francisco, 2027

A world where AI companies wield more influence than governments. 
Billion-dollar valuations depend on proprietary models and data. 
The culture worships 'disruption' and 'moving fast' while burying 
ethical concerns under layers of PR.

Power is measured in compute access, data ownership, and board seats.
Information is the ultimate weapon — leaked emails, model weights, 
internal Slack logs. Everyone performs their role: the Visionary Founder, 
the Loyal Engineer, the Ethical Voice.

Loyalty is to equity, not people. Madness looks like burnout, obsessive 
debugging, and public meltdowns on social media. Poison becomes sabotaged 
code, corrupted training data, or planted vulnerabilities.

The setting: gleaming offices in SOMA district hiding cutthroat politics. 
Demo days that make or break careers. Board meetings where empires shift.
All-hands meetings where truth is performed, never spoken.
