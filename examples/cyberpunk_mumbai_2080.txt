Cyberpunk Mumbai 2080 — The Neon Dharavi

Mumbai, 2080. Most of the old city is submerged due to rising sea levels, now known as the "Drowned Wards". Above it rise the "Sky-Chawls" — massive, gravity-defying arcologies connected by bioluminescent sky-bridges.

The city is ruled by Corporate Maharajas, CEOs of mega-conglomerates who have replaced the government. They live in the "Cloud Peaks", literally above the smog line. Below, in the neon-drenched alleys of New Dharavi, millions of "Data-Dalits" mine cryptocurrency with their own neural implants to pay for basic water credits.

Religion has merged with technology. "Digital Darshan" allows uploading consciousness to the Nirvana Server for a fee. Temples are server farms; priests are code-monkeys maintaining the karmic algorithms that determine your social credit score.

Cyber-rickshaws fly through holographic advertisements for "Instant Moksha" chips. Street food vendors sell synthetic vada pav made of algae paste, spiced with mood-altering chemicals.

The underworld is run by "Bhai-Bots", AI gangsters who enforce loansharking contracts executed on the blockchain. Resistance movements use ancient analog tech — paper notes and radio waves — to organize strikes against the algorithmic tyranny.

Atmosphere: Constant monsoon rain (acidic), neon lights reflecting on wet surfaces, cacophony of digital horns and temple bells, smell of ozone and frying spices.
