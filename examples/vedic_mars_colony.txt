Vedic Mars Colony — The Red Ashram

The year is 2150. The colonization of Mars is led not by corporations or governments, but by the "Aryavarta Interplanetary Alliance". The colony, situated in Valles Marineris, is designed based on Vastu Shastra principles.

Geodesic domes act as "Prana-Shields", protecting inhabitants from radiation. Inside, the red dust is kept at bay, and lush, genetically modified forests of Neem and Tulsi generate oxygen.

Society is organized into functional guilds, a meritocratic reinterpretation of the varnas. AI Gurus, hovering as holographic orbs, teach quantum physics through the lens of the Upanishads. Space travel is described as "Vimana Vidya".

Terraforming is treated as a "Yajna" (ritual sacrifice). Massive atmospheric processors chant low-frequency "Om" vibrations to stabilize the planetary core.

The aesthetic is "Solarpunk Sadhus". People wear saffron spacesuits with smart-fabric dhotis. Meditation pods are ubiquitous. The currency is "Karma Credits", earned through community service and innovation.

Conflict arises between the "Purists" who want to keep Mars a spiritual sanctuary and the "Earth-Bound" who want to strip-mine the planet for resources to send back to a dying Earth.

Key Elements: Fusion of high-tech and ancient spirituality, reverence for nature (even alien nature), community-centric living, pursuit of knowledge as a spiritual path.
